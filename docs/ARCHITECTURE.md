# AI Workflow

## 1. Purpose

The AI workflow defines how AI is used throughout the Living Brand Intelligence System.

The product must not behave like a single general-purpose chatbot where one large prompt generates an entire brand.

Instead, AI should operate through a structured sequence of specialized reasoning stages.

The core workflow is:

```text
ROUGH IDEA
    ↓
DISCOVER
    ↓
STRUCTURE
    ↓
STRATEGIZE
    ↓
CHALLENGE
    ↓
FOUNDER APPROVAL
    ↓
PERSISTENT BRAND CONTEXT
    ↓
NEW INFORMATION
    ↓
CHANGE DETECTION
    ↓
IMPACT ANALYSIS
    ↓
FOUNDER REVIEW
    ↓
BRAND EVOLUTION
