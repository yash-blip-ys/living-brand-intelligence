# Context Model

## 1. Purpose

The Context Model is the structured memory of the startup.

It is the foundation of the Living Brand Intelligence System.

The system must not rely on the LLM remembering previous conversations or store the startup as one large AI-generated summary.

Instead, important information about the startup must be represented as individual, structured, traceable context items.

The Context Model allows the system to:

- Understand what is known about the startup.
- Separate confirmed information from AI interpretation.
- Track uncertainty and assumptions.
- Track founder-approved strategic decisions.
- Preserve rejected ideas.
- Preserve decisions that were later replaced.
- Connect brand decisions to the context that supports them.
- Detect when new information may affect existing decisions.
- Explain why an important decision exists.
- Allow the startup and its brand to evolve without losing history.

---

# 2. Core Concept

The system must distinguish between three fundamentally different things:

### What do we know?

Facts and confirmed information.

### What do we think?

AI interpretations and unvalidated hypotheses.

### What have we decided?

Founder-approved strategic decisions.

These distinctions are critical.

For example:

```text
FACT
"College students struggle to find hackathon teammates."

INFERENCE
"Compatibility may matter more than simple teammate discovery."

HYPOTHESIS
"Students may prefer skill-based matching."

DECISION
"Position the brand around compatible team formation."
