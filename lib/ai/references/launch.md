# Launch messaging

- Lead with the audience’s immediate job and the product’s distinct approach. Do not lead with vague transformation language.
- Make the headline specific enough to be recognizable and simple enough to recall. The subheadline should explain value, not repeat the headline.
- Calls to action should name the next action and its immediate outcome. Match the promise to what the product actually does.
- Every proof point must trace to approved startup context. A hypothesis must remain visibly conditional.
- Social launch copy should sound like a founder explaining why the work exists, who it serves, and what is different now.
- Review launch copy against positioning, personality, voice, and visual direction before publication.

Example structure: Headline states the specific job; subheadline explains the approach; CTA names the next step; proof section cites only approved evidence.
