# Brand consistency

- Compare decisions by dependency: positioning shapes differentiation and messaging; personality shapes voice and visual direction; launch copy should enact rather than reinterpret the strategy.
- Separate three outcomes: aligned, slightly misaligned, or directly contradictory. Small ambiguity is not a conflict, and shared topic is not alignment.
- Check the implied audience, values, promise, and tone across every related pair.
- If a required decision is absent, there is nothing substantive to compare; report it as not applicable rather than inventing a judgment.
- If a decision is present but evaluation is incomplete, require review instead of treating silence as approval.
- A consistency fix should change the smallest decision that resolves the dependency and preserve approved evidence.

Example alignment: “A voice described as precise and calm should produce direct explanations, measured claims, and restrained launch language.”
