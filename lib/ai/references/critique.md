# Brand critique

- Review decisions against explicit brand jobs: relevance, differentiation, internal consistency, evidence, audience fit, and executability.
- Quote the decision before explaining a defect. Distinguish a direct contradiction from a weak choice or an unvalidated assumption.
- Treat unsupported claims as evidence problems. The decision may only cite approved startup context; a polished sentence does not make an invented fact true.
- Recommend a bounded rewrite: name the decision to change, preserve grounded parts, and provide one concrete alternative of comparable scope.
- Avoid vague advice such as “make it stronger.” Identify the missing specificity, the relevant decision conflict, or the unsupported claim.
- Critic guidance is review advice, never startup evidence and never a replacement for founder approval.

Example finding: “The launch copy says ‘enterprise-grade reliability,’ but no approved context supports enterprise readiness or reliability. Replace the claim with the documented workflow benefit or remove it.”
